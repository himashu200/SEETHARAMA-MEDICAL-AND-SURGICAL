Who We Are

A Reliable Pharma Dealer Serving Warangal Rural

SEETHARAMA MEDICAL AND SURGICAL is a pharmaceutical dealership based at Gorrekunta Village in Geesukonda Mandal, Warangal Rural District, Telangana. We deal in medicines, surgical instruments, disposables and general healthcare products.

Our goal is simple — to make genuine, quality healthcare products easily accessible to hospitals, clinics, retail pharmacies and individual customers in and around our region. Every product we supply is procured through authorised channels, so our customers can rely on both authenticity and fair pricing.

As a local business, we understand the needs of our community. Whether it is a routine prescription, a bulk order for a nursing home, or an urgent requirement for surgical consumables, we work to ensure timely availability and dependable service.
